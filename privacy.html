Privacy Policy

Sugam Hotels and Stays respects the privacy of every guest and visitor. We collect personal information only when required to process room bookings, answer enquiries, provide services and improve our guest experience.

Information may include your name, contact details, travel dates, room preferences and any special requests. This information is used to manage reservations, communicate with you about your stay, support operational needs and comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties. Limited information may be shared with trusted service providers who help us operate the hotel, such as payment processors, reservation tools, IT support and customer communications, subject to confidentiality obligations.

We use standard website analytics and cookies to understand visitor behaviour and improve site performance. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, although some website features may be affected.

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected or as required by law. You may request access, correction or deletion of your personal information by contacting reservations@sugamhotels.com.

By using our website or contacting our team, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this policy. This policy may be updated from time to time, and the latest version will be published on this page.
